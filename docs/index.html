01 / CHOOSE

Your tracks. Your selection.

Pick individual songs or select every track loaded from your Udio library or Suno workspace.

02 / RECORD

A queue that keeps going.

Record normal playback into separate WAVs. Completed tracks are remembered locally. Choose “Record again (new copy)” whenever you want a fresh recording; existing WAVs stay intact.

03 / KEEP

Files you can work with.

Standard stereo WAV files, saved in the folder you choose. No AssetRaider account or cloud upload.

FROM DOWNLOAD TO PLAYBACK

Three steps.
Then let it play.

No extension. No separate .NET install.

  1. 1

    Install and choose a site

    Choose your platform above and follow its install steps. Open AssetRaider, then select Udio or Suno.

  2. 2

    Sign in with normal Chrome

    Click “Sign in (normal Chrome)”. Sign in in the separate window and close it when finished.

  3. 3

    Select tracks and record

    Load your library, choose songs or Select all, pick a save folder and click “Record selected as WAV”. Keep the dedicated Chrome window open and the computer awake. Linux records silently through its dedicated audio channel.

A few things to know

Playback takes playback time

A four-minute track takes about four minutes to record. WAVs use roughly 11 MB per minute. The dedicated browser can stay behind your other windows.

WAV format, streaming quality

These are recordings of the audio you play, not original masters or native service WAV exports. Saving as WAV does not improve the source quality.

Local by design

Sign-ins use separate local Chrome profiles. Audio is captured from that browser process, not your microphone. Recordings stay in your chosen folder.

An unsigned beta

The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. You can inspect the source or build it yourself. Release notes & checksums ↗
